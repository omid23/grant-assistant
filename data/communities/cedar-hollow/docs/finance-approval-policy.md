# Finance approval policy (Cedar Hollow)

## Purchase approval

Purchases over $5,000 need director approval. Purchases over $25,000 need a council motion.

## Grant applications

Staff may prepare a grant application but cannot submit it. Every application is reviewed
and submitted by the finance manager, and matching funds must be confirmed before the
application is prepared.

## Grant funds

Grant funds are kept in a separate account per grant and reported quarterly to the funder.
