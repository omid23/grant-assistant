# Youth Leadership Program guidelines (Cedar Hollow)

The Youth Leadership Program runs September to June for members aged 14 to 21.
Weekly sessions are held at the community hall on Tuesday evenings.

## Reporting

The youth program report is due November 15, 2026. It goes to the program manager
and covers attendance, spending to date and any incidents.

## Spending

Food and transport costs can be claimed with receipts. Honoraria for guest speakers
are capped at $250 per session.
